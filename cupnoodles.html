Identity for Cupnoodles Museum

Client
Cupnoodles Museum / HSE Art and Design School
Year
2026
Category
Branding & Identity
Role
Art Direction & Identity System
01 / Concept & Purpose

Interactive Museum Experience

Designed as a fresh branding concept for the iconic Cupnoodles Museum, highlighting the history, creativity, and culinary innovation behind instant noodles.

02 / Execution & Form

Dynamic Graphic Identity

Combining playful typography, graphic noodle wave motifs, and vibrant packaging touchpoints to create a memorable museum identity system.

Dynamic Noodle Wave Motifs & Visual Language

Fluid line structures and expressive typographic rhythms reinterpret instant noodle strands into an energetic visual code, framing exhibition spaces, promotional posters, and interactive displays.

Playful Packaging & Merchandise System

The identity expands into custom souvenir packaging, cup containers, wayfinding, and museum store merchandise that engage visitors of all ages in a joyful, hands-on experience.
