01 / Cultural Exchange & Mission

International Cultural Exchange

The Japan Foundation is Japan's primary institution dedicated to international cultural exchange. The foundation promotes Japanese arts, language education, and cross-cultural understanding through global educational initiatives and artistic programs.

02 / Rebranding & Visual System

Minimalist Geometry & Spatial Balance

A comprehensive visual identity rebranding and brandbook design. The visual system balances traditional Japanese minimalist aesthetics with contemporary graphic systems across posters, publications, event collateral, and spatial applications.

01 / Brandbook & Identity Architecture

Establishing a unified typographic grid and modular system. The visual guidelines structure logo usage, color palettes, and typographic hierarchies to reflect cultural clarity and institutional prestige.

02 / Printed Publications & Event Collateral

Translating Japanese aesthetic principles—ma (negative space) and restrained geometric balance—into event announcements, exhibition catalogs, and cultural festival posters.

03 / Environmental Touchpoints & Merchandise

Extending the identity across branded souvenirs, tote bags, stationery, and spatial signage for cultural centers, unifying digital, print, and physical touchpoints.
