Identity for Fixed Expression

Client
Fixed Expression / HSE Design School
Year
2026
Category
Branding & Identity
Role
Art Direction & Visual System
01 / Context & Challenge

Custom Knitwear & Vintage Warmth

Fixed Expression (Устойчивое выражение) is a brand of custom knitwear that inspires a love for vintage items through the lens of a new era. It offers unique aesthetic pieces, original gifting options, and custom creations tailored for authentic self-expression.

02 / Concept & Solution

Knitting Charts & Stitch Grid

The visual identity is built on a graphic metaphor derived from knitting scheme charts and symbols mimicking hand stitches. It establishes a space where traditional craftsmanship finds a new, sustainable form—becoming a unique graphic code for self-expression.

01 / Pattern Grid & Visual Code

Reinterpreting traditional handicraft notations into a contemporary visual language. Abstract stitch symbols and pattern grid structures form the brand's primary graphic syntax across print and digital media.

02 / Tactile Packaging & Labels

Extending the stitch metaphor to custom woven labels, tactile kraft packaging, thread spools, and care guides that bridge vintage warmth with clean modern packaging design.
