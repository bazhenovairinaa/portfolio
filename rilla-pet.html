01 / Context & Challenge

New Furniture Piece for annki studio

The task was to design a new piece of furniture for the brand annki studio, taking their signature clover motif as a creative foundation. It was important to preserve the recognizable studio DNA: soft rounded volumes, solid proportions, and natural materials.

02 / Concept & Solution

Pet Bowl Stand with Accent Leg

The clover silhouette was reimagined as a three-legged pet bowl stand made of solid ash. One of the supports is engineered as a monolithic accent finished in smooth enamel, turning a utilitarian pet object into a full-fledged interior furniture piece.

Color Options

The accent leg comes in three enamel finishes designed to fit different interiors: muted sage green, warm terracotta, and deep chocolate.

Collection Fit

The bowl holder shares the soft volumes and tactile wood finish of annki studio's main furniture line, creating a consistent look throughout the home.
