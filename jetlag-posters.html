Posters JET LAG

Client
HSE Art and Design School
Year
2023
Category
Graphics & Posters
Role
Poster Design / Art Direction
01 / Context & Challenge

Speculative Adaptation Space

JET LAG is a speculative adaptation center designed for settlers arriving from different planets and galaxies. The center supports migrants through essential acclimatization stages: adapting to natural environmental conditions, mastering local culture and language, and overcoming psychological transition barriers.

02 / Concept & Solution

Thematic Series & Graphic Contrast

Developed within the Poster Design Module at HSE Art and Design School, the project features multiple distinct poster series—exploring welcome greetings for new arrivals, psychological milestones of unity, planetary orbital arrival calendars, and environmental acclimation diagrams.

01 / Welcome Protocols & Billboards

Introducing extraterrestrial arrivals to the adaptation center. High-contrast typography paired with deep space textures and planetary spheres sets an inviting tone across desert billboards and arrival terminals.

02 / Psychological Trust & Gestures

Translating trust, adoption, agreement, freedom, and unity into high-contrast stippled hand gestures—symbolizing the emotional bridge built between distinct galactic species during acclimatization.

03 / Greeting Cycles & Orbital Calendars

Greeting travelers across Andromeda M31 and Messier M74/M90 galaxies with specialized time-zone banners, paired with detailed planetary arrival calendars tracking orbital days for Gliese 875 D, Cancri E, and HD 209458.
