Book Mono-ha

Client
HSE Art and Design School
Year
2025
Category
Graphics & Editorial
Role
Book Design & Editorial Art Direction
01 / Context & Task

Создание книги

The task required choosing a theme for a comprehensive visual research project, conducting the in-depth investigation, and subsequently using this gathered material as the foundation to design and publish a book. For this project, I chose the Japanese art movement 'Mono-ha' as the core foundation.

02 / Решение и концепция

Визуальное исследование Моно-ха

Created as a comprehensive editorial project within the Multi-Page Print Publication course at the HSE Art and Design School. Leading the book design and art direction, the publication investigates the tactile dialogue among wood, stone, and glass, inviting quiet contemplation and an awareness of nature’s self-sufficiency.

01 / Structure & Principles

Reimagining editorial structure as an extension of the Mono-ha philosophy. The publication acts as a physical medium where unrefined matter, spatial tension, and human perception meet in quiet dialogue.

02 / Stone Installations

Exploring natural stone in its unformed state—where mass, gravity, and raw tactile texture interact directly with surrounding architectural planes and negative space.

03 / Wood & Organic Textures

Documenting timber, logs, and wooden structures. Unrefined surfaces and natural grain accentuate the transience, organic warmth, and physical presence of timber.

04 / Glass & Transparency

Investigating glass as a boundary between presence and void. Light reflections, transparency, and fragility create delicate spatial encounters with surrounding matter.

05 / Archive & Bibliography

A curated bibliography and visual catalog consolidating historical texts, theoretical essays, and archival documentary photography from the movement's pivotal years.
