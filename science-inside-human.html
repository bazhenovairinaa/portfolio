01 / Concept & Purpose

Chemistry Inside Us

Every chemical reaction in the world is far more than an abstract equation—it directly shapes our surroundings, moods, and biological states. Inside the human body, continuous atomic processes unfold every second, sustaining vital functions and defining physical well-being.

02 / Mechanics & Format

Augmented Poster Series

Designed to explain chemistry at the atomic level, the project links dynamic motion graphics with a series of physical posters through Augmented Reality (AR) tracking, bridging microscopic human biology with spatial digital experience.

Molecular Dynamics & Visual Language

Exploring the subtle chemistry operating within us. Graphic elements translate internal molecular reactions into tangible graphic forms, highlighting the direct connection between atomic activity and human life.

Interactive AR Tracking

By scanning each printed poster, AR spatial tracking reveals dynamic motion layers over the static graphics, providing an intuitive visual explanation of microscopic biological events.

Visual Contrast & Density

The visual identity pairs high-contrast orbital forms with stippled gradients and glowing energy centers, capturing the feeling of an expansive universe contained within human biology.
