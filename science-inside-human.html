01 / Context & Challenge

Моушн-дизайн с дополненной реальностью

Creating motion graphics with augmented reality for any theme. Showcasing how chemistry is directly connected to our lives through a series of posters and augmented reality.

02 / Решение и концепция

Наука и процессы внутри человека

Create science-themed posters to explain processes inside the human body. Every chemical action in the world is not just a formula in a textbook. It affects the air, water, our home, and the human body. We often do not see these processes, but they constantly happen around us.

Графика молекулярных процессов

Abstract orbits and particle fields translate invisible biological reactions into clear visual forms, showing the connection between atomic physics and human sensations.

Взаимодействие через AR

Scanning a printed sheet launches smooth motion graphics on the screen, giving the viewer an intuitive look at how particles move and interact inside the body.
