Packaging for the Cashmere Theater

Client
Cashmere Theater (Moscow)
Year
2025
Category
Packaging
Role
Art Direction, 3D Modeling & Project Lead
01 / Context & Challenge

Design-Tsekh Hackathon Sprint

Developed as part of the "Design-Tsekh" hackathon — an intensive 2-day team sprint. The task was to create an interactive premium gift gastro-box packaging concept for the private Cashmere Theater in Moscow, designed to set the audience's mood for a cultural evening even before the performance begins.

02 / Core Concept & Solution

Theater Begins with Anticipation

The packaging is not merely a box, but a carefully curated emotional journey across three sequential stages: Invitation — Anticipation — Aftertaste. Through a structured ritual of tactile touchpoints, printed inserts, and gastro-sets, the audience is guided into the production's atmosphere long before stepping into the hall.

Entrance into Experience & Guided Ritual

The box is designed for the theatergoer an hour before the performance, easing them into the atmosphere of the evening. The preparation begins with an instruction card on the gift bag detailing the route and guidelines. Inside, accompanying brochures placed between trays gradually immerse the guest in the production's mood.

Gastro-Sets & Sensory Engagement

Individual gastro-sets add deeper narrative context, enhancing perception and emotional engagement. The guest's mindset is prepared through a guided ritual combining curated flavors with brief textual prompts. This allows the theater to engage not just with the stage, but directly with the audience's senses.

Room for Growth: Eco-Friendly Cycle

The concept incorporates fully recyclable packaging. After the performance, guests drop the used packaging into a branded recycling container in the foyer. The theater manages the recycling process with clear messaging on the bin: "Thank you — this will be recycled."
