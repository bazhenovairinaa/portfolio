01 / Academic Context & Mechanics

Custom Typeface & OpenType Code

Developed as part of a coursework project at the HSE Art and Design School. Embedded with OpenType contextual alternates, the font automatically substitutes repeating glyphs in real-time, recreating the organic variance of handwritten script where no two characters are identical.

02 / Concept & Festival

Epistola — Culture of Letter-Writing

"Epistola" is an intellectual urban festival celebrating handwritten correspondence and the culture of genuine communication. In an era of digital noise and ephemeral messaging, we create a space where words regain weight, intimacy, and tactile presence.

Anatomy & Contextual Alternates

The letterforms merge structural discipline with the organic flow of ink on paper. Embedded with OpenType contextual alternates code, the font automatically substitutes repeating characters in real-time, recreating the organic variance of authentic handwriting where no two glyphs are identical.

Identity Scale & Architectural Interventions

When scaled up to street furniture, architectural construction hoardings, and billboard posters, the delicate ink-like lines transform into powerful graphic gestures, creating an immediate visual dialogue with urban commuters.

Tactile Touchpoints & Contemporary Stationery

To immerse attendees in the culture of letter-writing, the identity extends to physical objects: custom ink bottles, wax seals, collectible postage stamps, cloth tote bags, and archival folders designed for hands-on workshops.
