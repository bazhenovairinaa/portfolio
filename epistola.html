01 / Контекст и задача

Custom Typeface & OpenType Code

Developed as part of a typeface design course at the HSE Art and Design School, the task was to master font creation and build a comprehensive identity upon it.

02 / Решение и концепция

Epistola — Culture of Letter-Writing

"Epistola" is an intellectual urban festival celebrating handwritten correspondence and the culture of genuine communication. In an era of digital noise and ephemeral messaging, we create a space where words regain weight, intimacy, and tactile presence, enhanced by OpenType contextual alternates that recreate the organic variance of handwritten script.

Контекстные альтернативы и пластика

The plasticity of the letterforms evokes the smooth flow of ink on paper. Embedded with OpenType contextual alternates code, the font automatically substitutes repeating characters in real-time, recreating the organic variance of authentic handwriting where no two glyphs are identical.

Графический образ

The ink-like strokes are crafted from enlarged glyphs, referencing handwriting and translating into powerful graphic gestures that become an integral part of the identity.

Tactile Touchpoints & Contemporary Stationery

To immerse attendees in the culture of letter-writing, the identity extends to physical objects: custom ink bottles, wax seals, collectible postage stamps, cloth tote bags, and archival folders designed for hands-on workshops.
