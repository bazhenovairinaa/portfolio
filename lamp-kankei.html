Lamp Kankei

Client
HSE Art and Design School
Year
2025
Category
Object Design / Lighting
Role
Product Design / Prototyping
01 / Concept & Form

Natural and Industrial Balance

Kankei (关系 / 関係 — связь, отношение) — a table lamp that explores the interaction between raw wood and functional lighting. The project looks at how natural textures and engineered light can live together in one simple object.

02 / Visual Context

Mono-ha Principles

The project takes inspiration from the Mono-ha movement: focusing on the meeting of different materials, the role of empty space around the object, and the beauty of unprocessed surfaces.

Material Quality and Craft

Instead of decorative masking, the lamp highlights the natural qualities of wood, metal, and light. The prototype was made by hand — from cutting the block to final sanding.

Wood Selection

Siberian larch was chosen for its density and visible ring pattern. A light stain reveals the depth of the grain while preserving the tactile warmth of the surface.

Carving Details

The round openings were cut and finished with hand chisels. Subtle traces of tools were left visible on purpose to give the object an honest, tactile character.

Internal Light Source

A warm LED strip sits hidden inside the carved channel. The light shines downward onto the wooden base, creating soft reflections that highlight the relief of the grain.
