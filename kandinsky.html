01 / Контекст и задача

160 лет Василию Кандинскому

Created as part of the Poster Lab at HSE Art and Design School for the 160th anniversary of Wassily Kandinsky's birth. The task was to create a poster within one of the specific periods of Kandinsky's artistic career.

02 / Решение и концепция

Деформация под влиянием иррациональной формы

The visual logic is based on the deconstruction of Kandinsky's late-period artistic style. A geometric grid serves as a symbol of rational order, which undergoes intense deformation under the influence of irrational color dynamics, rhythmic lines, and expressive forms.
