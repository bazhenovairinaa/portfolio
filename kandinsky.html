01 / Context & Competition

Wassily Kandinsky 160th Anniversary

Created as part of the Poster Lab course at HSE Art and Design School to commemorate the 160th anniversary of Wassily Kandinsky. The posters were designed for the prestigious International Poster Biennale "Golden Bee" call for entries.

02 / Solution & Concept

Rational Grid vs. Irrational Form

The visual logic is rooted in a deconstruction of Kandinsky's late period. A geometric grid serves as a symbol of rational order, which undergoes dramatic physical deformation under the influence of irrational color dynamics, rhythmic lines, and expressive shapes.

Structural Deformation & Musical Synesthesia

The grid structure, initially disciplined and rational, folds and fractures across the canvas. Typography and organic lines interrupt the pattern, creating a graphic score that echoes Kandinsky’s philosophy of visual music: "Color is the keyboard, the eyes are the hammers, the soul is the piano with many strings."
