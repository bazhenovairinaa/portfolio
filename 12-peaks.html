Client

Year

Category

Role
Graphic Design / Print
01 / Concept & History

240 Years of Mountaineering

By 2026, for nearly 240 years, thousands of people have strived to conquer Earth's summits to test their limits and touch eternity. This calendar showcases the unique features of twelve of the world's greatest peaks while honoring the rich history of alpine exploration.

02 / Information Architecture

Comprehensive Mountain Profiles

Each month is dedicated to a single iconic summit, detailing its elevation, geographical location, primary climbing routes, first conquerors, historical records, average ascent duration, difficulty rating, and optimal climbing season.

Editorial Layout & Topographic Mapping

Every calendar sheet merges topographic vector mountain graphics with detailed expedition metrics. Key routes, high-altitude camps, and pioneer milestones are mapped alongside the monthly grid, functioning both as a calendar and a visual tribute to extreme alpine feats.

Bold Elevation Typography & Visual Contrast

Oversized typographic numbers represent each mountain's height in meters, creating a strong vertical anchor on every sheet. Color-coded routes and high-contrast stippled textures guide the eye through dense factual data.

Chronicles of Human Endurance

From Matterhorn and Mont Blanc to K2 and Everest, each spread highlights pivotal expedition dates, team records, and ascent timelines, connecting graphic design with real human achievement.
