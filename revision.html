Year

Category
Branding & Medical VR
Role
Art Direction & Visual System
01 / Context & Challenge

VR Rehabilitation for Motor Recovery

Revision is a Virtual Reality rehabilitation initiative supporting patients suffering from severe long-term immobility caused by neurological and physical trauma. The VR headset tricks the brain into perceiving that it is executing observed movements, exciting brain neurons and reactivating bodily motor functions.

02 / Concept & Solution

Mirror Neurons & Visual Pathways

By observing tailored VR recordings of physical movements, the patient's mirror neuron system is stimulated, initiating neural pathway reconnectivity. Once excited, these target motor units reduce pain and prevent muscle atrophy—a process reflected in a dynamic visual brand language.

01 / Rehabilitation Mechanics & Immersion

Immobilized patients put on a virtual reality headset and observe a pre-recorded sequence of physical actions. Visualizing movement activates mirror neurons in the motor cortex, stimulating neural pathways even when the physical body remains still.

02 / Neural Reconnection & Motor Recovery

As the mirror neuron system begins to re-establish connections, excited neurons trigger target motor units. This biological response helps reduce physical pain, reactivates motor function, and prevents long-term muscle atrophy.
