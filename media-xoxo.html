01 / Context & Challenge

Open Dialogue & Intimate Wellness

XOXO is an independent digital media platform dedicated to dispelling myths surrounding sex and dismantling cinematic tropes. Its primary mission is to improve intimate wellness, eliminate prejudice, and prevent negative personal experiences through honest, educated dialogue.
