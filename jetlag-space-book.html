01 / Concept & Metaphor

Moon Phases & Satellite Growth

The editorial narrative is structured around lunar phases: adaptation modeled as the progressive growth of a planetary satellite. As settlers move through spatial transition, the publication visually maps the gradual emergence of light, balance, and spatial orientation.

02 / Editorial Format

Multi-Page Spatial Publication

Combining high-contrast astronomical typography, tactile paper textures, and structured editorial grids, the publication guides readers through deep-space acclimatization protocols and cosmic migration narratives.

01 / Lunar Phases & Transition

Visualizing the transition from dark orbital shadow to illuminated lunar phase. Layout structures shift from dense cosmic blackness to clear typographic clarity, guiding migrants through spatial adaptation.

02 / Editorial Grids & Data

Rigorous typographical systems paired with stippled planetary maps. The editorial grid functions as an architectural anchor for settlers navigating deep-space acclimatization data.

03 / Integration & Balance

Reaching complete orbital alignment. High-contrast photographic spreads document physical artifacts, environmental guides, and the full culmination of the adaptation process.
