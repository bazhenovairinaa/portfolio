01 / Context & Audience

Studio of Contemporary Theatre for Teenagers

"I.T.D. Workshop" is a contemporary acting and performance studio created for teenagers aged 14–18 at the Children's Center of the Museum of Moscow. The studio offers an open laboratory space for self-expression, experimental play, and creative discovery.

02 / Design Metaphor & Mechanics

Stage Blocking Diagrams & Spatial Trajectories

The visual language translates directorial blocking notations into dynamic graphic layouts. Position points, trajectory vectors, color-coded stage zones, and theatrical cues serve as flexible graphic building blocks across digital media, posters, and merchandise.

Stage Mis-en-scène & Spatial Trajectories

The visual identity translates the physical movement of rehearsals into dynamic graphic layouts. Position points, movement vectors, color-coded stage zones, and director's cues serve as flexible graphic building blocks.

Subtle Theatrical Wit in Everyday Objects

The identity extends into personal items: socks labeled with "L." and "R." stage indicators, keychains with position marks, and coffee packaging created in collaboration with Surf Coffee.

The Backstage & Audience Atmosphere

Touchpoints frame the theatrical experience — from entrance wristbands and costume garment covers to water bottles printed with "Intermission" and sweatshirts bearing "Stage Exit".

Tactile Touchpoints & Exhibition Spaces

Everyday workshop materials — pencils, folders, phone cases, and laptop sleeves — seamlessly connect with large-scale interior museum screens, linking the studio's daily creative process with public exhibition spaces.
