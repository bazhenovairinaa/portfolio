01 / Контекст и задача

Студия современного театра для подростков

"I.T.D. Workshop" is a contemporary acting and performance studio created for teenagers aged 14–18 at the Children's Center of the Museum of Moscow. The task was to develop a distinct visual identity in the spirit of this workshop.

02 / Решение и концепция

Режиссерские мизансцены и траектории

The visual language translates directorial blocking notations into dynamic graphic layouts. Position points, trajectory vectors, color-coded stage zones, and theatrical cues serve as modular elements of the corporate identity.

Сценические мизансцены и пространственные траектории

The visual identity translates the physical movement of rehearsals into dynamic graphic layouts. Position points, movement vectors, color-coded stage zones, and director's cues serve as flexible graphic building blocks.

Театральная ирония в повседневных вещах

The identity extends into personal items: socks labeled with "L." and "R." stage indicators, keychains with position marks, and coffee packaging created in collaboration with Surf Coffee.

Атмосфера закулисья и зрительский опыт

Touchpoints frame the theatrical experience — from entrance wristbands and costume garment covers to water bottles printed with "Intermission" and sweatshirts bearing "Stage Exit".
