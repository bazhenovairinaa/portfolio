Brand Storefront for Tamburins

Client
Tamburins / HSE Design School
Year
2025
Category
Object Design & Spatial
Role
Spatial & Storefront Design
01 / Context & Challenge

Brand Context & Fragrance Aesthetics

Tamburins is a South Korean niche skincare and fragrance brand created by the team behind Gentle Monster. Renowned for its avant-garde visual language, architectural packaging, and non-standard marketing campaigns that resemble cinematic art films, the brand positions cosmetics as tactile art pieces rather than mere utility.

02 / Concept & Solution

Sculptural Showcase & Spatial Calm

The objective was to design a flagship storefront display that captures Tamburins' core philosophy—interweaving aesthetics, sensory perception, and daily ritual. By combining raw architectural textures with sculptural display forms, the storefront transforms retail window shopping into an immersive gallery encounter.

Architectural Podiums & Materials

Translating Tamburins' signature minimal aesthetics and natural textures into a spatial showcase. Products are elevated on geometric pedestals, creating a dialogue between raw structural elements and refined bottle silhouettes.

Lighting & Atmosphere

Echoing the brand's filmic marketing approach, the window display uses atmospheric lighting, natural material balance, and negative space to turn passersby into gallery visitors.
