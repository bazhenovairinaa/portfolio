Category

01 / Контекст и задача

Анимированный веб-плакат для атмосферы проекта

The task was to create an animated web poster that immerses the viewer in the atmosphere of the project. JET LAG is a speculative adaptation center designed for settlers arriving from different planets and galaxies.

02 / Решение и концепция

Плавная адаптация в новой среде

Adaptation is an essential part of every living organism when encountering new conditions, and the center makes this stage simpler and more comfortable. The web poster serves as an interactive portal dedicated to integrating beings into a new cosmic environment through motion and code.

View Live Web Poster ↗

Visual System & Graphical Language

The visual system relies on contrasting grain textures, pulsating spheres, and warm luminous nodes along the central axis, visualizing the process of restoring physical balance after interplanetary travel.

Keyframe Motion & Rhythm

CSS keyframe animations maintain continuous movement and stable performance across screens, visually guiding the viewer through states of disorientation, rest, and final acclimatization.
