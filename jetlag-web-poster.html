01 / Concept & Narrative

Cosmic Adaptation Center

JET LAG is a speculative adaptation center designed for settlers arriving from different planets and galaxies. The web poster serves as an image-driven portal dedicated to integrating beings into a new cosmic environment. Because adaptation is essential for any living organism entering unfamiliar conditions, the center makes this transition seamless and comforting.

02 / Execution & Technology

100% Pure HTML & CSS Animation

Developed as part of the Design and Back-Jamming program at the HSE Art and Design School. The entire interactive poster — including all smooth motion paths, fluid particle transitions, and atmospheric light pulses — is executed entirely through native HTML and CSS code without heavy script libraries.

Intergalactic Integration & Visual Language

The graphic system uses high-contrast stippled silhouette imagery, pulsating organic solar spheres, and glowing orange energy nodes along the central axis, symbolizing the recalibration of physical and mental equilibrium for extraterrestrial travelers.

Fluid Web Motion & Responsive Adaptation

Designed to maintain smooth framerates across all screen aspect ratios, the keyframes guide the visitor through key adaptation phases: euphoria, disorientation, acclimatization, and final integration.
