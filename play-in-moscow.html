Thermoses for Play in Moscow

Client
Play in Moscow / HSE Design School
Year
2026
Category
Object Design & Product
Role
Product Design & Modeling
01 / Context & Mission

Prize Fund for Urban Quests

Developed as part of a creative laboratory sprint to design new concepts for the "Play in Moscow!" gift fund. Upon completing city tourist routes and stamping their map at all checkpoints, young participants receive a collectible reward at tourist information centers.

02 / Product Concept

Children's Character Thermoses

We proposed expanding the gift fund with a line of children's thermoses. Each thermos visually embodies a unique character from the route universe, turning a utilitarian object into a friendly companion. Streamlined shapes without sharp corners meet 3+ safety standards, while soft-touch coatings and carrying-handle packaging turn each piece into a proud token of achievement.

Ergonomics and Safety

Each thermos visually brings to life a distinct character from the "Play in Moscow!" route universe. Designed with smooth, corner-free contours that comply with 3+ safety regulations, the objects combine hypoallergenic materials with a velvety soft-touch finish for comfortable everyday handling by children.

Gift Packaging

The compact gift box featuring an integrated textile handle allows children to comfortably carry their reward directly from the tourist information center. This thoughtful unboxing presentation creates a proud sense of accomplishment, highlighting the value of completing the urban exploration route.
