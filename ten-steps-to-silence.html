01 / Context & Album Presentation

Ten Steps to Silence Debut Album

Event poster system created for the debut album presentation of "Ten Steps to Silence" at the DOM Cultural Center. The graphic language translates complex avant-garde acoustic performance into a high-contrast visual system.

02 / Graphic Metaphor & Sound Granulation

Acoustic Texture & Taiko Drum Impact

The visual identity is built on a metaphor of sound granulation, where a dense vibrating pattern simulates an improvised noise canvas and emerging acoustic layers. Bold contrasting accents act as a visual counterpart to Taiko drum beats, structuring the surrounding sonic chaos.

Sound Granulation & Vibrating Patterns

The dense particle pattern acts as a graphic canvas reflecting musical improvisation and acoustic granularity. Micro-elements shift and vibrate across the page, capturing the physical feel of sound waves radiating through space.

Taiko Rhythm & Tactile Artifacts

Subtle Taiko drum strike metaphors guide the eye across album covers, vinyl labels, concert lighters, and urban city light displays, creating an immersive, multi-sensory brand ecosystem for concertgoers.
