01 / Контекст и задача

Презентация дебютного альбома

The poster series was created for the debut album presentation of the 'Ten steps to silence' band at the DOM Cultural Center. The task was to translate the experimental acoustic music into a concise and contrasting poster format.

02 / Решение и концепция

Акустическая фактура и ритм

The visual language relies on granulated noise textures that convey free musical improvisation. Accent contrast elements operate on the principle of sharp drum hits, structuring the rhythm of the composition.
