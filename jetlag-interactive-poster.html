01 / Concept & Purpose

Cosmic Adaptation Center

JET LAG is a speculative adaptation center created for settlers arriving from different planets and galaxies. The center helps travelers overcome key integration challenges, including adapting to new environmental conditions, learning cultural and linguistic nuances, and addressing psychological adjustment.

02 / Mechanics & Format

Interactive Exploration Screen

Conceived as an image-driven interactive web poster, the project allows users to navigate and explore a newly encountered cosmic space through dynamic motion system layers, temporal shifts, and responsive visual mechanics.

Visual Metaphor & Motion

The visual identity translates spatial disorientation into dynamic graphic forms. Interactive triggers guide visitors through acclimatization protocols, easing the psychological transition into unfamiliar planetary settings.

Dynamic Responsive Elements

Pulsating celestial forms and responsive typographic layers simulate time zone distortion, bridging intergalactic transit mechanics with real-time browser interaction.
