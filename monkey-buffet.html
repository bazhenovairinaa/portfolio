Identity for the Monkey Buffet Festival

Client
Monkey Buffet Festival (Lopburi)
Year
2026
Category
Branding & Identity
Role
Art Direction / Graphic Design
01 / Контекст и задача

Annual Festival in Lopburi

Monkey Buffet Festival takes place every year on the last Sunday of November in Lopburi, Thailand. Locals set out tables with fruits for the monkeys to thank them for attracting travelers to the town. The task was to develop a memorable visual identity for this festival that captures its vibrant character and playful atmosphere.

02 / Решение и концепция

Веселье обезьян и изобилие фруктов

The visual identity is built on organic fruit silhouettes and expressive character graphics. Through soft contours, vibrant tropical colors, and dynamic layouts, the design comes alive, transforming local traditions into a cohesive, immersive brand experience.

Пластика и визуальный язык

The visual language scales dynamically across the entire project space, using organic fruit shapes and playful character interactions to build an engaging, cohesive brand environment that brings the festival's festive energy to life.
