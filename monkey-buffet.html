Identity for the Monkey Buffet Festival

Client
Monkey Buffet Festival (Lopburi)
Year
2026
Category
Branding & Identity
Role
Art Direction / Graphic Design
01 / Context & Culture

Annual Festival in Lopburi

Monkey Buffet Festival takes place every year on the last Sunday of November in Lopburi, Thailand. Locals set out tables with fruits for the monkeys to thank them for attracting travelers to the town.

02 / Core Concept & Solution

Visual Metaphor & Dynamic System

The visual identity is based on simple fruit shapes and character graphics. The key graphic element is a curious monkey looking for treats, combined with bright tropical colors and clean layouts.

Logo & Graphic Language

The brand system combines rounded organic contours with simple typography. The logo mark subtly brings together a silhouette of a monkey and the shape of tropical fruits, making it easy to recognize and adapt across materials.

City Presence & Packaging

The style works cleanly across city touchpoints: street entrance banners, billboards in local parks, social media announcements, and canned fruit drinks made for visitors.

Merchandise & Printed Matter

The line of items includes simple apparel, temporary sunscreen stencil stickers for sunbathing, and a small booklet with tips on how to interact with monkeys safely.

Accessories & Souvenirs

Small brand objects complement the collection: glossy acrylic keychains, embroidered caps, beach towels, and packaging stickers for delivery boxes.
