01 / Concept & Mission

Intergalactic Adaptation Center

JET LAG is a speculative adaptation center designed for settlers arriving from various planets and galaxies. The center supports migrants through essential acclimatization stages, including adapting to new environmental conditions, learning cultural and linguistic nuances, and navigating psychological transition challenges.

02 / Digital Platform & Features

Multi-Page Web Platform

The multi-page website functions as a comprehensive digital portal where settlers can explore the company's core vision, purchase specialized adaptation merchandise, book consultation sessions, navigate spatial environments, and access interactive media resources.
